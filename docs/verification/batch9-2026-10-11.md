### Vermont

- [11 V.S.A. Chapter 25](https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/fullchapter/11/025):
  LLC foreign application/authority §§ 4112–4113, fees § 4012, reports § 4033,
  sanctions §§ 4119–4120. Registration **$155**, foreign annual report **$170**,
  due within three months after fiscal year end (March 31 for calendar-year firms).
- [11A Chapter 15](https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/fullchapter/11A/015),
  [Chapter 1](https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/fullchapter/11a/001), and
  [Chapter 16](https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/fullchapter/11a/016): profit
  corporation authority §§ 15.01/15.03, sanctions § 15.02, fees § 1.22, annual
  reports § 16.22. Registration **$155**, foreign report **$250**, within two and
  one-half months of fiscal year end (March 15 for calendar-year firms). Domestic
  report fees ($45 LLC / $60 corporation) are not used in foreign guides.
- Isolated transactions outside repeated similar business have **no fixed day
  limit** for either entity. Solicitation requires out-of-state acceptance.
- Both sanction statutes restrict affirmative proceedings, counterclaims,
  crossclaims and affirmative defenses, while preserving ordinary defense and
  contracts. Civil penalty **$50/day, maximum $10,000 per year**, plus fees and
  other sanctions; no exact amount is inferred without relevant facts.
- [SOS foreign registration](https://sos.vermont.gov/business-services/business-filings/foreign-registration)
  requires home existence certificates dated within **90 days LLC / 30 days
  corporation**. No additional online filing fee is stated.
- [§ 1655](https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/section/11/015/01655), effective
  July 1, 2025, governs consenting agents, current contact information and changes.
  An individual resident or authorized business with a Vermont place of business
  may serve; the LLC designated notification office is a separate concept.
- [SOS fees](https://sos.vermont.gov/business-services/fees-statutes/) corroborate
  foreign fees and the **$155 foreign LP** filing stub. The legislative website
  labels its online text an unofficial convenience copy of Vermont Statutes Annotated;
  it is retained as an official legislative publication, with that notice preserved.

### Virginia

- [LLC Chapter 12](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacodefull/title13.1/chapter12/):
  §§ 13.1-1052 application, 1059 exemptions, 1057–1058 sanctions, 1015 agent,
  1005 filing fee, 1062/1064 annual fee/late penalty. Registration **$100**;
  annual registration fee **$50**, late fee **$25**. LLCs do not file annual reports.
- [Stock corporation Chapter 9](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacodefull/title13.1/chapter9/):
  §§ 13.1-757/759 authority/application, 758 sanctions, 763 agent, 775 report.
  Annual reports have **$0 filing fee**; registration assessments are separate.
- Initial corporate filing **$25** plus entrance fee **$50 per 25,000 authorized
  shares or fraction** up to one million shares, then **$2,500** above one million;
  **$75 is a minimum**, not a universal total. Current SCC759 and the separately
  retrieved §§ 13.1-615.1/616 agree with those initial charges.
- **Annual corporate fee discrepancy disclosed:** both the full chapter and the
  separately retrieved [§ 13.1-775.1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title13.1/chapter9/section13.1-775.1/)
  say **$50 for up to 5,000 shares, then $15 per additional 5,000 or fraction,
  maximum $850**. The [SCC February 2026 assessment schedule](https://www.scc.virginia.gov/media/sccvirginiagov-home/business-home/business-faqs/annual-registration-fees/an_fee.pdf)
  says **$100, then $30 increments, maximum $1,700**. The guide preserves both
  figures and requires confirmation of the actual CIS assessment; `fee=None`
  prevents treating either schedule as one flat amount. Disclosure documents the
  conflict; it does not establish that either conflicting source is authoritative
  for the actual checkout. Late payment is the greater of **$10 or 10%**.
- Default annual deadline is the last day of the Virginia registration/authorization
  anniversary month, following the SCC record. Isolated safe harbor **30 days**;
  conditional motion-picture exemption is left to manual review.
- Knowing unauthorized participants, not automatically the entity, may face
  **$500–$5,000 each**: LLC members/managers/employees and corporate
  officers/directors/employees. Contracts and defenses remain valid.
- [SCC checklist](https://www.scc.virginia.gov/businesses/cis-help/foreign-registration-checklist/)
  requires certified articles and applicable amendments/transaction documents,
  certifications **no more than 12 months old**. Good-standing certificates alone
  are insufficient. Individual agents need qualifying roles or State Bar membership;
  residency alone does not establish eligibility.
- [§ 50-73.17](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title50/chapter2.1/section50-73.17/)
  supports the **$100 foreign LP** filing stub.

### West Virginia

- Official substantive PDFs for [LLC Article 10](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/pdf/31B-10/)
  and [corporation Article 15](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/pdf/31D-15/) establish
  authority, exemptions, certificates and consequences. Initial LLC **$150** /
  corporation **$100**, corroborated by current LLF-1/CF-1 forms and SOS guidance.
- Isolated safe harbor **30 days**. LLC § 31B-10-1003(b) expressly treats income-
  producing real/tangible property as business, subject to exclusions. Corporate
  § 31D-15-1501(d) also addresses contract performance, torts and defective-product
  injury; guide notes retain those fact-specific triggers for manual review.
- Private agents/offices are **optional**, under §§ 31B-1-108 / 31D-15-1507.
  SOS statutory service and forwarding are distinct from private appointment,
  under §§ 31B-1-111 / 31D-15-1510. Contact details must remain current.
- [§ 59-1-2a](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/59-1-2A/), effective **July 1, 2026**:
  default **annual $25**, due **June 30 at 11:59 PM**, beginning the calendar year
  after registration. Subsection (d) describes a January 1–July 1 window; subsection
  (e) supplies the June 30 deadline, also stated in the July 2026 SOS report form.
- **Conditional biennial $50**: five consecutive timely annual years plus good
  standing. Elect with the fifth timely report; already eligible businesses may
  elect January 1–July 1, 2027. Skip the next calendar year; first biennial report
  in the second calendar year after election, then every two years. Eligibility
  can be revoked after delinquency/loss of good standing; five new timely annual
  years are required before reelection. This is an alternative, not an extra
  unconditional annual obligation.
- Current **annual late fee $50 / biennial $100**, including LLCs. Eligible
  veteran/active-duty businesses may have first four annual fees waived; filing
  deadlines remain. Corporate back fees/taxes/penalties are fact-specific; no
  unsupported flat nonqualification fine is encoded for either entity.
- Home existence certificate required; no numerical freshness rule is inferred.
  LLF-1's **90-day delayed-effectiveness** limit is not certificate freshness.
  The mistakenly retrieved LLD-10 download is excluded as annual-report authority.
  Foreign LP **$150** is a filing-only stub.

### Wisconsin

- Official [LLC Chapter 183](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/statutes/statutes/183.pdf)
  and [corporation Chapter 180](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/statutes/statutes/180.pdf),
  corroborated by [DFI fees](https://dfi.wi.gov/Pages/BusinessServices/BusinessEntities/Fees.aspx)
  and Forms 521/21. PDFs display **Updated 23–24 Wis. Stats.**; that compilation
  label is retained, not rewritten as a certified 2026 compilation.
- LLC registration **$100**, foreign annual report **$65 online / $80 paper**
  (including $15 surcharge), **January 1–March 31 beginning the next calendar
  year**. Domestic anniversary-quarter rules do not apply to foreign entities.
- Corporate authority **minimum $100**, plus **$3 per $1,000 or fraction of
  Wisconsin-represented capital above $60,000**, § 180.0122(1m)(u). Foreign
  annual reports **minimum $65 online / $80 paper**, with capital-increase fees;
  variable annual total remains `None`. Form 21 supplies the business/property
  ratio and capital worksheet; no flat universal total is asserted.
- LLC isolated transactions have **no fixed day cap**, outside repeated similar
  transactions (§ 183.0905); corporations retain **30 days** (§ 180.1501).
- **LLCs do not attach a home good-standing certificate** under reviewed
  § 183.0903/Form 521. Corporations require an authenticated certificate of
  status/existence **within 60 days**, § 180.1503(2)/Form 21; articles alone are
  not a substitute. Do not infer six months from unrelated agent provisions.
- §§ 183.0902(6) / 180.1502(5): for each unauthorized year or part, fees/charges
  that would have applied plus **50% or $5,000, whichever is less**, payable before
  registration. Form 521 worksheet uses **$97.50 per prior report calendar year**
  ($65 plus 50%), plus $100 registration. Exact recovery requires historical facts.
- Eligible agent and physical Wisconsin registered office required; mailbox/
  answering services alone are insufficient. Foreign LP **$75** is a filing stub.
