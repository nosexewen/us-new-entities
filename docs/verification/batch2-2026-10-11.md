## Alabama

Official [§ 10A-1-7.01](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/code-of-alabama?section=10A-1-7.01)
requires foreign filing entities to register and attach a name reservation
certificate. [§ 10A-1-7.04](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/code-of-alabama?section=10A-1-7.04)
and current SOS applications establish application content and attachments.
[§ 10A-1-7.23](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/code-of-alabama?section=10A-1-7.23)
allows a fee equal to the registration fee for each delinquent year after more
than 90 days doing business. Assessment and year attribution remain manual.

The $25 name-reservation base fee is separate from the $150 qualification fee;
online processing charges are excluded. Current agent requirements in
[§ 10A-1-5.31](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/code-of-alabama?section=10A-1-5.31)
exclude virtual-only and forwarding-only offices. The SOS's official HB 230
notice confirms repeal of corporate annual reports effective October 1, 2024,
superseding the $10 report row in its 2023 fee schedule. Business privilege tax
and licensing are separate; the model does not infer that tax obligations are
zero. Current Title 10A does not supply the old corporate activity list in the
reviewed provisions; generic activity exemptions remain manually screened.

## Alaska

Working official statutory URLs:

- [Chapter 10.06](https://www.akleg.gov/basis/statutes.asp?media=print&secStart=10.06&secEnd=10.06.995)
- [Chapter 10.50](https://www.akleg.gov/basis/statutes.asp?media=print&secStart=10.50&secEnd=10.50.995)

AS 10.06.718 and 10.50.720 impose a 30-day completion limit for isolated,
nonrepeated transactions. The LLC statute lists member/manager meetings;
unspecified internal affairs are conservatively left for review rather than
automatically applying the corporation's broader internal-affairs exemption.
AS 10.06.710 and 10.50.700 provide up to $10,000 per calendar year or portion,
with the entity-specific historical fees, taxes and penalties also reviewed.
These are discretionary/enforcement amounts, not automatic exact bills.

AS 10.06.811 and 10.50.760 require reports before January 2 in years matching
Alaska registration parity. Commerce uses January 2 and lists $247.50 foreign
reports with late charges from February 2; the statutory delinquency and mailing
language is separately disclosed, and the guide uses the earlier January
deadline. Statutes condition a six-month report on receipt of a department form;
Commerce's FAQ says foreign entities do not file its Initial Report. Both
statutory and agency instructions are documented.

Current LLC form 08-0497 requires a home-jurisdiction good-standing certificate
or equivalent. Current corporation form 08-414 instead includes a good-standing
attestation and does not list that certificate as an attachment. No invented
certificate age is imposed. The agent FAQ permits resident individuals or
authorized corporations, explicitly excluding LLC/LP/LLP entity agents.

## Arizona

Official [§ 29-3905](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/29/03905.htm) gives LLC isolated
transactions no fixed day limit; [§ 10-1501](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/10/01501.htm)
requires corporations to finish within 30 days without repeated like transactions.
ACC guidance confirms foreign registration requirements. Corporate
[§ 10-1502](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/10/01502.htm) adds up to $1,000 plus
historical fees and applicable penalties; the $1,000 is not an annual multiplier.
It is not applied to LLCs. Other LLC sanctions remain a manual assessment.

[§ 10-1622](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/10/01622.htm) requires the ACC-assigned
annual corporate report and 20% of the unpaid fee per month or fraction: $9
on a $45 report. Extension requests do not defer payment. The ACC explicitly
says LLCs do not file annual reports. Both foreign applications require certified
formation documents/amendments and a certificate of existence within 60 days.
Form directories link the actual LLC L025 and corporate C018 filings.

## Arkansas — primary statutory review remains open

Current SOS forms FL-01 and F-01, fee pages and the 2025 business guide establish
fees, street-address agent requirements and home-state certificates within
30 days. The 2026 corporation franchise tax form confirms May 1, capital-based
0.3% tax, minimum $150 for stock corporations and $300 without authorized stock.
The LLC $150 amount is supported by the SOS foreign-LLC schedule and guide.
Late tax charges are separate from qualification sanctions; a 2025 temporary
waiver is not applied to later years.

Current statutory reproductions reviewed:

- [§ 4-38-905](https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-4/subtitle-3/chapter-38/subchapter-9/section-4-38-905/): LLC isolated transactions have no fixed day limit.
- [§ 4-38-913](https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-4/subtitle-3/chapter-38/subchapter-9/section-4-38-913/): historical fees/penalties plus up to $5,000 for each twelve-month period or part, with enforcement consequences.
- [§ 4-27-1501](https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-4/subtitle-3/chapter-27/subchapter-15/part-a/section-4-27-1501/): corporate isolated transactions must finish within 30 days.
- [§ 4-27-1502](https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-4/subtitle-3/chapter-27/subchapter-15/part-a/section-4-27-1502/): corporate historical charges and up to $5,000 per year or partial year.

The official Act 1041 download failed twice. Justia is labeled as a third-party
reproduction, and the Georgia-specific acceptance is not extended to Arkansas.
The guide is populated but retains an open primary statutory verification flag.
Older SOS Chapter 32 references do not override current Chapter 38 rules.

## Colorado

The legislature's current download index points to
[official 2026 Title 7](https://olls.info/crs/crs2026-title-07.htm), retrieved
successfully. §§ 7-90-801–803 establish authority, 30-day isolated transactions,
and enforcement. § 7-90-802 separates the prescribed fee capped at $100 per
calendar year/part from the civil penalty capped at $5,000 without a yearly
multiplier. Historical assessments, interest and costs remain unquantified.

§ 7-90-501 and the SOS FAQ establish the reporting window and entity-specific
month; the current fee schedule is $25, not the older $10. § 7-90-701 and the
SOS's July 1, 2025 guidance establish agent consent, ID or alternative passcode
verification, entity good standing and the physical business location. The
2026 statute's employer EEO-1 provisions beginning July 1, 2027 are noted as a
future review obligation.

## Scope and validation

The registry schema requires LLC, C-Corp and LP entries. LP filing fees/forms
are included from the official schedules to maintain that contract, but this
batch does not claim a full LP statutory review: Alaska and Arizona LPs are
not assigned the LLC/corporate report, Arkansas's LP report is listed without
a fee, and Colorado LP reporting-entity eligibility remains manual. LP-specific
unreviewed exemptions and sanctions are not automatically inferred from LLCs.

No exact qualification penalty is manufactured from maximum amounts; monetary
components requiring applicant history or assessment remain unresolved.
