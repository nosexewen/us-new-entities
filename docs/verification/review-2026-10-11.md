## California

Reviewed California Legislative Information Corp. Code §§ 191, 2105, 2203,
17708.02, 17708.03, 17708.07, 17708.09, 15901.02(ai), 15909.02, 15909.03,
15909.07 and 15909.08; Gov. Code §§ 12186, 12188, 12190; and R&TC §§ 19135,
19141. These resolve the prior 403 gap. The corporation's court-assessed $20/day
willful-business sanction remains different from the LP's $20/day, $10,000 cap;
the LLC provisions do not import either schedule. LLC/LP evidence must be issued
within six months. All three types have nonrepeated 180-day isolated-transaction
safe harbors; income-producing property remains an LP-specific trigger.
The existing SOS/FTB guidance review dates remain October 10.

## Illinois

Live official PDFs: [LLC 45.5](https://www.ilsos.gov/content/dam/publications/pdf_publications/llc455.pdf)
item 13 requires authentication within 60 days;
[BCA 13.15](https://www.ilsos.gov/content/dam/publications/pdf_publications/bca1315.pdf)
item 12 requires certified articles as amended, authenticated within 90 days.
[LP 902](https://www.ilsos.gov/content/dam/publications/pdf_publications/lp902.pdf)
requires a current/recently authenticated partnership certificate without a
numeric age limit. Item 8 separately requires an original good-standing
certificate within 30 days for an entity general partner not registered in Illinois.

[Foreign BCA 14.05 worksheet](https://www.ilsos.gov/content/dam/publications/pdf_publications/bca1405f.pdf)
establishes capital allocation, 0.001 annual rate ($25 pre-exemption minimum),
$10,000 liability exemption from January 1, 2025, and the earlier exemption
periods. It also specifies 10% late tax penalty and 2% monthly/fraction interest
($1 minimum), neither computed when tax due is zero. These formulas and the
separate $75 filing fee are now documented. Applicant values and historical
periods still require assessment; no fixed tax total is invented. The initial
qualification worksheet is separate from the annual-tax worksheet.

## New York

[DOS beneficial-owner disclosure](https://dos.ny.gov/beneficial-owner-disclosure)
expressly states that effective January 1, 2026, disclosures cover nonexempt
LLCs formed under foreign-country law and authorized in New York. US-state-formed
LLCs are outside that class. Ordinary guide scope, variable newspaper charges,
regulated-activity consents and tax nexus are now explicit notes rather than
unresolved source flags. Existing authority, publication and biennial citations
remain in the module. This does not certify compliance for a foreign-country LLC.

## North Carolina

[Current SOS forms catalog](https://www.sosnc.gov/forms/by_title/_Business_Registration)
links the actual B-09, L-09 and LP-04 downloads, now used by StateForm.
B-09 item 10 and L-09 item 8 require authenticated original existence evidence
less than six months old; photocopies are not accepted. LP-04 requests formation
and partner records but no attached existence certificate. Fees confirmed by
the forms: $250 corporation/LLC and $50 ordinary LP. The guide quotes standard
paper fees and excludes optional payment/priority charges; it promises no queue
duration. Six calendar months remains a documented day-model limitation, not an
unverified legal rule or an invented 180-day exception.

## Washington

[WAC 434-112-085](https://apps.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=434-112-085)
sets standard foreign registration $180 plus applicable back fees, annual report
$70 and delinquency $25. The guide's registration quote is explicitly for
standard paper service; an optional online total is not asserted.
[WAC 434-112-080](https://apps.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=434-112-080)
sets $100/three-working-day expedite, $150 front-counter same-day subject to
hours/quantity, and 15-business-day ordinary mailed-paper processing.
[SOS annual reports](https://www.sos.wa.gov/corporations-charities/business-entities/maintain-business-compliance/annual-reports)
states last day of the formation/registration month, up to 180 days early;
early filing does not shift expiration. The actual record supplies the applicant
date. Conditional solicitation/remote-worker facts remain manual screening cases.
