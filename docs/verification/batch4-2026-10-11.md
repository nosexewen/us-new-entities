## Kansas

Current [K.S.A. 17-7931](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch17/017_079_0031.html)
requires foreign covered entities to register and make a good-standing statement
under penalty of perjury. The former 90-day certificate attachment wording is
not imposed. Repealed §§ 17-7301 and 17-76,121 are not used as current authority.
[§ 17-7932](https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch17/017_079_0032.html)
provides the reviewed activity exclusions and 30-day isolated-transaction limit;
[§ 17-7925](https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch17/017_079_0025.html)
and SOS FA instructions support the resident-agent and physical-address rules.

§ 17-76,126 bars LLC proceedings until registration and payment of historical
fees/penalties. § 17-7307 additionally includes corporate taxes. Actual arrears
remain manual. § 17-7937 permits an Attorney General action to enjoin unregistered
foreign covered entities; no fixed monetary sanction is inferred from that section.
§ 17-76,139(d) and § 17-7505 establish biennial foreign LLC/corporate reports due
April 15 in years matching Kansas application parity. Current ILC and IFP forms
confirm $90 online/$110 paper and possible historical reports with late registration.

Noted discrepancy, resolved by disclosure: current SOS FA lists $115 for all
foreign applicants. The [February 27, 2026 regulations](https://sos.ks.gov/publications/register/Volume-45/Issues/Issue-07/02-12-26-53865.html)
list $100 foreign-entity application base in K.A.R. 7-17-1, plus $10 services and
$5 technology paper charges, consistent with $115. K.A.R. 7-50-1 separately
lists $75 for foreign LLC application/recording. The guide preserves that figure
for SOS confirmation rather than silently equating it with the FA charge.

## Kentucky

Official [KRS 14A.9-010](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=44318)
requires authority and lists safe harbors, including 30-day isolated transactions.
Subsection (6) separately requires authority/qualification for state contracts;
ordinary activity exemptions do not erase that contract eligibility requirement.
[§ 14A.9-020](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=36569)
provides $2 per day without authority, litigation restrictions and preservation
of contracts and defense rights. Days and additional charges remain manual.

§ 14A.9-030 and the current SOS FBE application require a valid-existence
representation, not an attached home-state certificate with an invented age
limit. Agent consent must be signed or separately supplied. § 14A.4-010 and FBE
instructions require the registered office/eligible agent and communications
contact; the applicant cannot be its own agent. Legacy LP agent exceptions need
entity-specific confirmation. § 14A.6-010 requires annual reports January 1–June 30
starting the year after authority. SOS fees/FBE establish $90 qualification and
$15 annual reporting. The uniform qualification/report provisions support all
three schema entity types, subject to the noted legacy agent exception.

## Louisiana

Official [R.S. 12:1342](https://legis.la.gov/Legis/Law.aspx?d=76297),
12:1343 and 12:1345 govern LLC authority, exclusions and applications.
Corporations use [R.S. 12:301](https://legis.la.gov/Legis/Law.aspx?d=76472),
12:302 and 12:304, rather than importing the domestic Business Corporation Act's
numbering. Both reviewed safe-harbor lists retain 30-day isolated transactions.
Financial-industry exceptions and property operations are not generalized.

R.S. 12:1354 and 12:314 restrict judicial demands while preserving contracts and
defense rights, and allow recovery of historical fees/taxes/penalties. No fixed
maximum is invented. R.S. 12:315 is expressly repealed and is not used to impose
an old officer penalty. R.S. 12:1350 and 12:308 supply foreign agent/office
requirements. Form 972 and corporate § 12:304(E) confirm home-jurisdiction
certificate evidence within 90 days. R.S. 12:1350.1 and 12:309 require reports
on the qualification anniversary; corporate first reporting starts the next year.

The [dated SOS fee schedule](https://static.sos.la.gov/shared/fee_changes_2026.pdf)
effective October 1, 2026 sets **$185 LLC / $155 corporation qualification** and
**$35 annual reports**. Current corporation form 326 agrees with $155. Older
LLC form 972 quotes $150, and older agency report guidance quotes $30; those
figures are explicitly superseded and retained as a disclosed discrepancy.
Credit cards add $5; optional expedite
adds $35 for 24-hour handling or $60 priority. Historical arrears require the
applicable historical rates, not blind multiplication of the new charge.

## Maine

Official [Title 31 Chapter 21](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/31/title31ch21.pdf)
§ 1622 requires LLC foreign qualification and home-jurisdiction evidence issued
no more than 90 days before delivery. § 1623 lists exemptions including 30-day
isolated transactions. § 1629 provides $500 per unqualified year or portion and
an Attorney General recovery/injunction remedy; the LLC court-access restriction
specifically concerns proceedings to collect debts. Actual assessment is manual.
§§ 1665 and 1680 establish $150 foreign reports due January 1–June 1 starting
the next year, $50 late filing and $250 qualification.

Official [13-C § 1501](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/13-C/title13-Csec1501.html)
retains corporate 30-day isolated transactions. § 1502 specifies $500 per
unqualified year/portion and corporate litigation restrictions. § 1621 and
§ 123(1)(DD-1), (EE) establish $150 annual foreign reports and $50 late filing;
qualification costs $250. Current MLLC-12/MBCA-12 forms confirm 90-day certificate
evidence. Title 5 § 105 and the forms establish agent designation, consent and
physical address. Domestic $85 reporting prices are not used for these guides.

## Massachusetts

Official [M.G.L. c. 156C, § 48](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXXII/Chapter156C/Section48)
requires LLC registration within ten days of starting business, home-jurisdiction
evidence, resident-agent consent and $500 registration/report base fees.
Corporations Division guidance specifies the registration-anniversary report
deadline. [§ 54](https://malegislature.gov/laws/generallaws/parti/titlexxii/chapter156c/section54)
provides a fine up to $500 for each failure year, preserves contracts/defense
rights, and bars maintaining actions during the failure. LLC activity exemptions
are not inferred from the separate corporation act; unsupported activities
continue to produce manual review. No initial certificate age is invented from
the separate 90-day amendment/reinstatement instructions.

Official [c. 156D, § 15.01](https://malegislature.gov/laws/generallaws/parti/titlexxii/chapter156d/section15.01)
expressly treats real-estate ownership/leasing and labor activities as business,
and lists corporate safe harbors. Its isolated-transaction exemption has **no
fixed day limit**. § 15.02 provides historical late fees/interest/penalties and an
administratively determined monthly penalty, with a conditional first-ten-days
exception; no unsupported dollar ceiling is asserted. § 15.07 establishes
corporate registered-office/agent eligibility. The official foreign corporation
guide confirms filing within ten days and § 16.22 reporting within 2½ months of
fiscal year-end; failed direct § 16.22 downloads are not counted as statute reads.

The [official fee schedule](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/corporations/general-information/corporations-filing-fees.htm)
lists $400 corporate paper registration, $375 fax base, $125 timely paper annual
reports/$150 late, and $100 electronic timely report base. Published expedited
charges are $15 at the $375 level, $10 at $100, and $20 for $500 LLC filings.
Guides identify the filing method and preserve base versus surcharge totals.
