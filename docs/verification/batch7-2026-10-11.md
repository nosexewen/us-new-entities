## North Dakota

- LLC authority/application: NDCC 10-32.1-74/75; exemptions: 10-32.1-82;
  consequences: 10-32.1-84. The exemption list includes bank accounts, internal
  affairs and nonrepeated isolated transactions completed within 30 days, but
  does not enumerate solicitation or interstate commerce. Income-producing
  real/tangible property triggers qualification except specified excluded holdings.
- Corporate authority/application: 10-19.1-134/135; exemptions: 10-19.1-143;
  consequences: 10-19.1-142. Corporations have an out-of-state-acceptance
  solicitation exemption and a 30-day isolated-transaction exemption. Interstate
  commerce remains individual review; income-producing property has its own trigger.
- LLC registration is $135, corporate registration $145. Annual reports are $50
  for LLCs, due November 15; foreign corporate reports are $25, due May 15.
  First reports are due in the calendar year after authority. August 1 is the
  domestic corporate deadline and is not imported into the foreign guide.
- Farming/ranching and authorized livestock entities have additional restrictions
  and special deadlines; these guides cover ordinary LLC/corporate operations.
- LLC home certificate of status and corporate authenticated certificate of good
  standing/existence are required; no unsupported numeric freshness is added.
  Agent requirements are reviewed through Chapter 10-01.1 and entity statutes.
- Entity civil penalties can reach $5,000, and participating persons can separately
  face up to $1,000; fees, injunctions, interest and costs require manual assessment.
- The official LLC PDF scrape duplicated some lines and truncated fiduciary-holding
  text. The complete Justia chapter was used as a secondary cross-check of
  10-32.1-82, confirming its exemptions. The official PDF remains the cited
  primary source; direct curl download returned 403 despite successful research
  service retrieval. The failed section-specific Justia URL is excluded.

## Ohio

- LLC foreign registration: R.C. 1706.511; exemptions: 1706.512; consequences:
  1706.515. Isolated transactions outside repeated similar transactions have no
  statutory day cap. The model uses its explicit no-day-limit setting.
- Corporate licensing: 1703.03/04. Section 1703.02 excepts corporations solely
  engaged in interstate commerce, with specified machinery-service coverage.
  LLC bank-account/internal-affairs/ordinary-solicitation/isolated-transaction
  exemptions are not copied to corporations.
- Foreign registration/licensing is $99 for both requested types. Forms 617 and
  530A are retrieved. Corporations require good standing/subsistence dated within
  90 days before application; the LLC form/statute does not prescribe that attachment.
- Ordinary LLCs and for-profit corporations have no regular SOS annual report.
  The official FAQ and maintenance page distinguish professional associations,
  LLPs, nonprofits and charities; taxes, name renewals and agent changes are separate.
- LLC debt-collection proceedings require effective registration; prescribed fees,
  injunctions, interest and court costs require review. Corporate 1703.28 forfeiture
  ranges from $250 to $10,000 and may be remitted. Section 1703.29 adds a $250
  court-access forfeiture and specified percentage/cure requirements. Form 530A
  distinguishes business before and after October 15, 2009; statutory tax arrears
  are not assumed universally due under obsolete tax regimes.
- Ohio requires an eligible statutory agent and Ohio address. A PO box alone or
  commercial mail receiving agency is insufficient; the form permits the specified
  rural-route/box combination, which the guide notes.

## Oklahoma

- Current primary provisions were retrieved from OSCN: 2043, 2048, 2049, 2055,
  2055.2, 1130, 1132, 1134, 1137 and 1142. Current SOS forms/fees and Tax Commission
  guidance corroborate administrative requirements. The archived Senate Title 18
  PDF contains superseded fee amounts and is excluded as current authority.
- LLC registration is $300; nonrepeated isolated transactions must finish within
  30 days. Income-producing property has an LLC-specific qualification trigger.
  Corporate exceptions are narrower: purely interstate operations are excepted,
  while the solicitation exception includes outside acceptance/shipment and
  stock/sample restrictions. Ordinary solicitation remains manual review.
- Corporate qualification is 0.1% of maximum Oklahoma invested capital, minimum
  $300, with no fee base above authorized capital (1142(A)(13)). The displayed
  $300 is explicitly a minimum.
- LLC annual certificate is $25 on its registration anniversary (2055.2).
  Failure to file/pay within 60 days after the due date affects registration.
  Secretary of State agent service costs an additional $40 annually on July 1
  only when the LLC uses that agent; a private additional agent avoids that fee.
- Corporations keep the Secretary of State as agent and may add a private agent.
  The $100 annual fee is due July 1, collected by the Tax Commission, and continues
  after franchise-tax filing ended with tax year 2023. The statutory suspension
  provision applies if unpaid before September 1; Form 200-R is current guidance.
- 1142(A)(17) separately requires an anniversary invested-capital certificate until
  fees have been paid on total authorized capitalization. Additional fees are
  0.1% of excess invested capital subject to the statutory limit, not a flat LLC
  annual-certificate fee.
- LLC good-standing evidence is dated within 60 days per Form 0083. Corporate
  existence and financial statements are dated within six months under 1130.
- Corporate 1134 fines range $200–$500 per offense, with separate agent fines and
  specified post-notice daily certificate/fee penalties. Section 1137 restricts
  court access and requires applicable arrears. LLC 2048 supplies court consequences
  without a fixed monetary fine. Monetary estimates remain manual.
- OSCN CiteID 552968 (2043) and 552969 (2055.2) are marked as being superseded on
  November 1, 2026 and are used for the October 11 review. CiteID 365007 expressly
  says its version will go into effect November 1 and is excluded as current law.
  Future-effective language is disclosed rather than applied early.

## Oregon

- LLC authority/exemptions: ORS 63.701/707; consequences: 63.704. Corporate
  authority/exemptions: 60.701/707; consequences: 60.704. Both retain a 30-day
  nonrepeated isolated-transaction exemption and out-of-state-acceptance solicitation.
- Foreign authority and annual renewals cost $275 for both entity types. The
  current SOS forms page links the retrieved Business Registry Fee Schedule;
  its printed revision date is July 2013. Retrieved application forms corroborate
  authority pricing. The domestic $100 renewal is not used for foreign entities.
- Annual reports are due by the anniversary under 63.787/60.787. Eligible agents
  maintain an actual Oregon street-address business office under 63.721/60.721.
- Evidence is a certificate of existence current within 60 days, unless the home
  official provides the qualifying freely searchable registration database;
  agency forms request a web-verifiable home registry number for this alternative.
- Unauthorized-operation consequences include court restrictions and fees that
  would have applied during years/parts of unauthorized business. These sections
  do not supply a fixed daily fine, and valid contracts/defenses are preserved.

## Pennsylvania

- The official Chapter 4 establishes foreign registration (15 Pa.C.S. 411/412)
  and common exemptions (403). Isolated transactions outside similar transactions
  have no fixed day limit; the model does not invent a 30-day cap.
- LLC and corporate foreign registration cost $250. Section 412 and DOS guidance
  require the registration and docketing statements, not home good-standing or
  articles attachments.
- Pennsylvania requires a registered office, or the commercial registered office
  provider alternative under 109/411(f), without a separate mandatory private
  agent appointment. The guide describes the physical-office/CROP distinction.
- Annual reports began in 2025 under 146, cost $7 for ordinary for-profit entities,
  and start the calendar year after registration. Corporations are due June 30;
  LLCs September 30. DOS says administrative termination for missing reports begins
  with reports due in 2027, six months after the deadline; a terminated foreign
  registration must reregister rather than retroactively reinstate.
- Foreign corporations must publish registration/intention notice under 4124 in
  two newspapers of general circulation, one a legal journal if possible. Notice
  may precede or follow filing; retain proofs with minutes, not at DOS. Publication
  charges vary, and the corporate requirement is not applied to LLCs.
- Section 411 bars maintaining proceedings until registered while preserving
  contracts, defenses and liability limitations. It supplies no fixed monetary
  qualification fine; estimates remain manual.
