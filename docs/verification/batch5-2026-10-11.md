## Michigan

- LLC: MCL 450.5002 requires authority; 450.5008 lists exemptions, including a
  nonrecurring isolated transaction completed within 30 days. Filing is $50.
- Corporation: MCL 450.2011 and 450.2012 separately establish authority and the
  equivalent 30-day safe harbor. CSCL/CD-560 (July 2025) confirms $60 admission:
  $10 filing plus $50 initial admission under MCL 450.2060 and 450.2062. Later
  increases in shares attributable to Michigan may incur additional charges.
- Both July 2025 application instructions require home-jurisdiction good standing
  dated no earlier than 30 days before receipt.
- LLC statements are due February 15; authority after September 30 skips the
  immediately following deadline (450.4207). Corporate reports are due May 15;
  corporations authorized January 1 through May 15 skip that calendar year's
  report (450.1911). Current report fees are $25; the statutes specify $15 after
  September 30, 2027, rather than making that future rate effective now.
- MCL 450.5007 and 450.2055 impose $100–$1,000 per calendar month within a five-year
  lookback and a $10,000 company cap. LLC participant penalties, recoverable fees,
  interest and court costs remain separate. Exact totals require manual assessment.
- LP filing is $10 under MCL 449.2107. MCL 449.1902 permits an elected private
  agent; the shared mandatory-agent summary describes LLC/corporate filings.

## Minnesota

- LLC authority: 322C.0802; exemptions: 322C.0803; consequences: 322C.0808–0809.
  Income-producing real or tangible personal property specifically triggers LLC
  qualification except the enumerated fiduciary holdings. The 30-day isolated
  transaction exemption is preserved. Interstate commerce and out-of-state order
  acceptance are not automatically treated as listed safe harbors.
- Corporate authority and exemptions: 303.03/303.06. Corporate property facts are
  not given the LLC-specific automatic trigger. Monetary penalties under 303.20
  are discretionary: up to $1,000 plus up to $100 per month or fraction.
- Official SOS schedule: LLC authority $185 mail/$205 online or in person;
  corporate authority $200 mail/$220 online or in person. Base guide fees use mail.
- Annual renewal by December 31 starts the year after registration (322C.0208,
  303.14 and 5.34). LLC renewal is free; foreign corporate renewal is $115 mail
  or $135 online/in person. Corporate fees are not copied from domestic corporations.
- Applications attest home-jurisdiction organizational compliance; no unsupported
  home-state certificate attachment or numeric freshness requirement is invented.

## Mississippi

- LLC statutory reproduction: 79-29-1003 application, 1015 exemptions, 1013 court
  consequences and 1203 fees. Corporate reproduction: 79-4-15.01 authority and
  exemptions, 15.02 consequences, 15.03 application and 1.22 fees.
- Both types retain the 30-day nonrecurring isolated-transaction safe harbor.
  Both require authenticated home-jurisdiction existence evidence; the reviewed
  sections do not state a numeric freshness limit.
- SOS schedule corroborates $250 foreign LLC registration and $250 annual report,
  versus $500 foreign profit corporate authority and $25 annual report. SOS annual
  report guidance requires online filing January 1 through April 15. Domestic LLC
  free reporting is not applied to foreign LLCs.
- Late qualification is $10 per covered day, capped at $1,000 for each year,
  under LLC 1203(1)(g) and corporate 15.02(d). Yearly allocation is manual; there
  is no single lifetime $1,000 cap or unsupported exact calculation.
- Chapter 79-35 addresses, consent and service rules were read separately.
- The official SOS 2010 LLC PDF is excluded as current authority: newer statutory
  provisions differ, including reserved fields and fees. Justia is clearly labeled
  a reproduction. Primary statutory verification remains an open limitation.

## Missouri

- LLC authority/application: 347.153; exemptions/sanctions: 347.163. Corporate
  authority/exemptions: 351.572; sanctions: 351.574; application: 351.576.
  Both have 30-day isolated transactions; neither enumerates out-of-state order
  acceptance as an automatic safe harbor.
- SOS LLC-4 (January 2025) and Corp-42 confirm filing prices $105/$155 and
  home-jurisdiction certificates within 60 days. Prices include the $5 technology
  surcharge; the corporation statute's $150 base is not presented as the payable
  administrative total. The published fee schedule is dated June 30, 2015; its age
  is disclosed and current application instructions corroborate qualification fees.
- Both unauthorized LLCs and corporations face fines **not less than $1,000**.
  No numeric upper bound is fabricated, and the minimum is not called an exact total.
- Corporate initial report: within 30 days of qualification under 351.120(3).
  Subsequent reports: qualification month or valid changed month, by month end.
  Annual reports are $20 online/$45 paper. An eligible 351.122 biennial election
  replaces annual filing, in years matching formation/qualification parity, at
  $40 online/$90 paper. These rates include the statutory technology surcharge.
- Corporate reports are not applied to LLCs. The LLC guide directs separate
  review of entity-specific ongoing obligations rather than claiming blanket exemption.

## Montana

- LLC authority/exemptions: 35-8-1001; consequences: 1002; application: 1003;
  annual report: 35-8-208. LLC sanctions are $5 per covered day, capped at $1,000
  for each year, requiring manual yearly allocation.
- Current corporate registration: 35-14-1502/1503; exemptions: 1505; annual report:
  1621. Section 1502 does not specify a daily monetary fee. Neither the LLC
  daily penalty nor repealed Chapter 35-1 corporate law is imported.
- Both types have 30-day isolated transactions and additional state/public-contract
  rules. Missing registration can make public contracts voidable; the LLC notice
  and cure provision does not become a universal 30-day grace period.
- Current SOS schedule lists $70 qualification for either type, with $50 per named
  LLC series. Reports are due January 1 through April 15 starting the next year.
  The schedule waives timely LLC/corporate report fees and charges $35 after April
  15. Guides explicitly require confirmation of the waiver for the filing year.
- Applications attest organizational compliance and existence in the home
  jurisdiction. Numeric certificate freshness or attachments are not invented.
- Agent appointment/consent and address definitions: 35-7-105 and 35-7-102.

LP entries retain independently sourced basic filing fees/forms and conservative
manual screening. LP exemptions, reporting, sanctions and agent exceptions are
explicitly disclosed as outside this batch's full review. Empty LP reporting lists
are not claims that no obligations exist. Taxes, licenses, optional expedition,
professional entities and fact-specific penalties require separate assessment.
