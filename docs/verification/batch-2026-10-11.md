# Georgia, Illinois, New York, North Carolina, and Washington

Relevant official source content was reviewed on **October 11, 2026** using
Firecrawl search/scrape. Direct statute and instruction scrapes requested a live
fetch (`maxAge: 0`); search-returned official page content supplied additional
filing tables. Tavily's usage cap prevented using that service. Search excerpts
and third-party law mirrors were used only to locate primary sources, not as
verification of current governing statutes.

[Retrieval metadata](batch-2026-10-11-retrievals.json) records source URLs,
scrape identifiers, response status, and retrieved content lengths.

This batch adds all three modeled entity types (ordinary LLC, business corporation,
and ordinary LP). It replaces coming-soon guides for these five states, but does
not declare every rule issue resolved. Each module exposes concrete remaining
issues to the audit and rendered guide. Monetary sanctions with unresolved inputs
remain unquantified, rather than silently becoming zero or an invented total.

## Georgia

- [Foreign entity guide](https://sos.ga.gov/how-to-guide/how-to-guide-register-foreign-entity):
  the LLC, corporation, and LP application sections each quote $235 ($225 filing
  fee plus $10 service charge). Corporate applicants need an original home-state
  existence certificate dated within 90 days. The guide does not list that
  document for LLC/LP applicants, so their instructions request confirmation.
- [Annual registration guide](https://sos.ga.gov/how-to-guide/how-file-annual-registration):
  January 1–April 1, starting the year after foreign qualification; its current
  fee table quotes $60 for these three types and $25 for late annual filing. The
  physical Georgia street-address agent requirement appears in the instructions.
- [Business FAQ](https://sos.ga.gov/page/business-division-faq): more than 30 days
  of unregistered business triggers $500; LLCs/LPs also owe missed fees. The FAQ
  distinguishes the annually repeated LLP penalty from the ordinary LP penalty.

**Limits:** older SOS tables/FAQ show different online annual-registration totals.
The guide's $60 is displayed with that conflict disclosed. Statutory safe-harbor
text was not reviewed in this batch, so the model does not automatically exempt
bank accounts, interstate activity, or isolated transactions in Georgia. These
facts require manual review. Full statutory consequence review remains open.

## Illinois

- Official [LLC](https://www.ilsos.gov/publications/business-services/llc.html),
  [corporation](https://www.ilsos.gov/publications/business-services/dfc.html), and
  [LP](https://www.ilsos.gov/publications/business-services/lp.html) tables:
  $150 base foreign application for each type; annual report $75 for corporation
  and LLC, $100 for LP. Corporate qualification can require additional charges.
- [Corporate application BCA 13.15](https://www.ilsos.gov/content/dam/publications/pdf_publications/bca1315.pdf):
  certified articles as amended, authenticated within 90 days; PO-box-only
  registered office is unacceptable; $150 is the minimum, not an all-in quote.
- [LLC Act](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ILCS/details?ActID=2290&ActName=Limited+Liability+Company+Act.&ChapAct=805+ILCS+180%2F):
  § 45-47 has a 120-day nonrepeated isolated-transaction safe harbor. § 45-45
  specifies $2,000 plus $100/month or fraction after failing to qualify within
  60 days, with historical fees/penalties separately owed.
- [Corporation § 13.75](https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/080500050K13.75.htm):
  a 120-day isolated-transaction safe harbor.
  [§ 13.70](https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/080500050K13.70.htm)
  has the greater-of percentage/$200-plus-monthly sanction and historical charges.
- [LP § 903](https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/080502150K903.htm):
  30 days for a nonrepeated isolated transaction; income-producing property is a
  trigger unless excluded. [§ 907](https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/080502150K907.htm)
  preserves contracts/defense while restricting maintenance of proceedings.
- [LP annual report § 210](https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/080502150K210.htm):
  deliver within the 60 days preceding the first day of the anniversary month.

**Limits:** corporate franchise-tax exemptions, historical allocation, and late
assessment require actual inputs and current instructions. LLC/LP application
certificate freshness is not guessed. The statute's corporation/LLC safe-harbor
lists do not expressly list interstate commerce; that activity remains a manual
review case for those types rather than borrowing the LP list.

## New York

- DOS foreign [LLC](https://dos.ny.gov/application-authority-foreign-limited-liability-companies),
  [corporation](https://dos.ny.gov/application-authority-foreign-business-corporation),
  and [LP](https://dos.ny.gov/application-authority-foreign-limited-partnerships-0)
  application instructions: fees $250, $225, $200, respectively; home-jurisdiction
  existence evidence dated within one year. A corporation already doing business
  needs State Tax Commission consent.
- DOS [publication guidance](https://dos.ny.gov/certificate-publication-foreign-limited-liability-company-0)
  and the foreign LP application instructions: LLC/LP publication within 120 days,
  plus a separate $50 Certificate of Publication and variable newspaper charges.
  There is no corresponding ordinary-corporation requirement in this model.
- DOS [biennial statement guidance](https://dos.ny.gov/biennial-statements-business-corporations-and-limited-liability-companies):
  corporation/LLC statement every two years in the authority filing month, $9;
  do not impose this filing on an ordinary LP.
- Senate [LLC § 803](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/LLC/803) and
  [corporation § 1301](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/BSC/1301): reviewed
  limited enumerated safe harbors. LP safe-harbor questions remain manual.
- Senate [LLC § 302](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/LLC/302),
  [corporation § 305](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/BSC/305), and
  [LP § 121-104](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/PTR/121-104): statutory
  Secretary of State designation with optional private agent. The audit therefore
  does not require hiring a private agent to qualify.
- Senate LLC § 808, corporation § 1312, and LP § 121-907: unauthorized-business
  litigation restrictions with contracts and defense preserved.

**Limits:** taxes, consents, special publication exceptions, and current LLC
transparency reporting must be assessed separately. A qualification guide is not
a complete tax or beneficial-ownership compliance determination.

## North Carolina

Official legislature chapters [55](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/ByChapter/Chapter_55.html),
[55D](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/ByChapter/Chapter_55D.html),
[57D](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/ByChapter/Chapter_57D.html),
and [59](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/ByChapter/Chapter_59.html)
were retrieved and their relevant sections read.

- LLC/corporation foreign authority $250; ordinary LP registration $50.
- LLC annual report $200, April 15 beginning the next year. Corporate report
  due the 15th day of the fourth month after fiscal-year-end, $25 paper or $18
  statutory electronic fee. Ordinary LPs must not inherit LLP/LLLP annual reports.
- All three foreign statutes have a six-calendar-month isolated-transaction
  exception. The current day-only model cannot represent that exactly, so such
  activity requires manual review rather than substituting 180 days.
- Corporation/LLC sanction $10/day with $1,000 cap per year or partial year plus
  arrears; LP $500 plus costs and arrears. No lifetime cap or compound estimate is
  substituted for the statute's assessment.
- Registered office must have an NC street address and eligible agent.

**Limits:** the SOS portal failed automated retrieval. StateForm source links
point to the applicable official statutory application requirements; current SOS
application downloads, certificate freshness, service charges, and processing
queues must be confirmed before filing.

## Washington

- [Uniform Business Organizations Code](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=23.95&full=true):
  common foreign registration rule (§§ 505–510), 30-day nonrepeated transaction
  exception (§ 520), consenting in-state agent, and annual report (§ 255).
- Official [forms/fees](https://www.sos.wa.gov/corporations-charities/business-entities/filings-forms-information),
  foreign LLC/LP online registration instructions: $180 plus online processing
  for foreign registration; existence evidence dated within 60 days.
- [Annual-report instructions](https://www.sos.wa.gov/corporations-charities/business-entities/online-filing-instructions/file-annual-report-multiple-entity-types-online):
  listed profit-entity fee $70 including online processing; $25 delinquency fee.
  Use the actual annual expiration date; filing may begin 180 days before it.
- § 505 preserves contracts and defense but restricts maintenance of actions and
  assesses historical fees/penalties. No unreviewed universal fine is invented.

**Limits:** registration and report channel totals must be confirmed. The order
solicitation safe harbor also limits local performance; the coarse activity enum
cannot establish that condition, so solicitation remains a manual review case.
Unspecified employee duties are not automatically treated as exempt remote work.
