## Connecticut

Official [Chapter 613a](https://www.cga.ct.gov/current/pub/chap_613a.htm)
§§ 34-275a–34-275b require foreign LLC registration and a home-jurisdiction
certificate of existence, including the certificate requirement added in 2024.
§ 34-275d exempts listed activities and isolated transactions outside a course of
similar transactions, without a fixed day limit. § 34-247k requires reports
January 1–March 31 starting the following year; § 34-243u sets $120 qualification
and $80 report fees. § 34-243n permits a foreign LLC to designate the Secretary
of the State instead of a private agent.

Official [Chapter 601](https://www.cga.ct.gov/current/pub/chap_601.htm)
§§ 33-920–33-922 require corporate authority and home-jurisdiction evidence.
The corporate isolated-transaction exemption retains its 30-day completion
limit. § 33-617 combines $100 qualification filing with $285 license, and $150
annual report with $285 recurring license. § 33-953 uses the first annual
report's anniversary for subsequent reports, while license renewal has its own
authority-anniversary month-end deadline. The guides direct applicants to the
assigned state record rather than copying the LLC March deadline to corporations.

Both § 34-275a(g) and § 33-921(d) specify $300 per month or part-month without
registration, plus historical fees, taxes, interest and penalties. Qualification
within 90 days exempts the monthly penalty, not the underlying registration or
historical charges. Assessment is manual; no annual multiplier is invented.
The older 2017 LLC form is not used as current certificate instructions.

## Hawaii

Official [HRS § 428-1003](https://www.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol08_Ch0401-0429/HRS0428/HRS_0428-1003.htm)
and [§ 414-431](https://www.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol08_Ch0401-0429/HRS0414/HRS_0414-0431.htm)
retain the 30-day isolated-transaction limit. The LLC rule additionally treats
income-producing real or tangible personal property as transacting business,
subject to its exclusions. That trigger is not copied to the corporation rule.
§ 428-1008 and § 414-432 address litigation restrictions and historical fees and
penalties; neither reviewed provision supplies an invented fixed-dollar maximum.

DCCA's foreign LLC and corporation instructions confirm $50 filing plus $1
archives, optional $25 expedite, and home-jurisdiction evidence no more than
60 days old. The malformed generic partnership paragraph on the foreign LLC
web page is not applied to LLCs; the entity-specific FLLC-1 form controls.

§§ 428-210 and 414-472 establish anniversary-quarter reporting, with the first
report following the registration year. The
[July 1, 2026 DCCA release](https://cca.hawaii.gov/breg/third-quarter-hawaii-annual-business-reports-due-2026/)
confirms $12.50 online for LLCs and profit corporations and $10 per delinquent
year. A release with a 2019 deadline is excluded from current fee evidence,
regardless of its migrated 2026 update timestamp.

## Idaho

Official [§ 30-21-502](https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/title30/t30ch21/sect30-21-502/)
requires foreign filing entities to register, bars maintaining proceedings until
registration, and preserves contracts and defense rights. It does not specify
a fixed monetary penalty. [§ 30-21-505](https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/title30/t30ch21/sect30-21-505/)
lists the reviewed safe harbors, including isolated transactions without a day
limit. These uniform provisions also support the LP screening rule.

[§ 30-21-213](https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/Title30/T30CH21/SECT30-21-213/)
requires annual reports by close of business on the anniversary month's final
day, starting one year after registration. The annual report fee is **$0 online
through SOSBiz**, with **$20 processing for manual paper filings** under Idaho
Secretary of State business filing guidelines. The user explicitly confirmed
these figures on October 11, 2026; the prior report-fee limitation is cleared.
This confirmation supplements the retrieval record rather than claiming a new
source download. SOS forms establish $100 base registration plus $20 manual
processing. The current foreign-registration FAQ requests home-state evidence
dated within 90 days; applicants must confirm type-specific workflow attachments.
§§ 30-21-402 and 30-21-404 establish agent designation and consent.

## Indiana

Official [Indiana Code 2026 Title 23](https://iga.in.gov/laws/2026/ic/titles/23)
§§ 23-0.5-5-2–23-0.5-5-3 establish uniform registration and home-jurisdiction
certificate requirements. State Form 49459 specifies evidence issued within
60 days, $125 paper filing and the Indiana agent address/consent details.
§ 23-0.5-5-5 expressly retains a 30-day isolated-transaction limit; it is not
removed merely because other uniform-code states omit a day limit.

§ 23-0.5-5-2(f) provides a civil penalty not exceeding $10,000, collectible by
the Attorney General. It is not multiplied by unregistered years; the actual
assessment remains manual. § 23-0.5-2-13 establishes biennial reporting on the
SOS schedule; INBiz describes anniversary-date filing with month-end grace
before past due. Assigned due year must be confirmed in the business record.

Noted discrepancy, resolved by disclosure: § 23-0.5-9-34 states $20 electronic
for-profit report filing; INBiz publishes $32 online. Both state $50 paper,
which is the modeled report amount. No unsupported explanation of the $12
difference is asserted. § 23-0.5-9-26 lists $75 electronic foreign registration
and $125 paper; the guide documents both and directs users to verify checkout.

## Iowa

Official [2026 Chapter 489](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/489.pdf)
§§ 489.902–489.903 govern LLC registration and 90-day certificate evidence.
§ 489.905 contains an isolated-transaction exemption without a day limit.
The current LLC reporting section is **489.212**, following recent transfers;
reports are due January 1–April 1 in odd years, starting the first such year
after registration. SOS instructions set $30 online/$45 paper.

Official [§ 490.1502](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/490.1502.pdf)
and the current corporate registration form establish authority and 90-day
certificate evidence. [§ 490.1505](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/490.1505.pdf)
contains no isolated-transaction day limit; the obsolete corporate 30-day test
is not used. [§ 490.1621](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/490.1621.pdf)
requires reports January 1–April 1 in even years, starting the first such year
after registration. SOS lists a $60 profit-corporation report fee, not the LLC
online discount. Qualification is $100 for each requested type.

The reviewed qualification sections preserve contract validity and defense
rights while barring maintaining proceedings before registration. Monetary
sanctions are not inferred from silence. LLC § 489.912 permits an Attorney
General injunction. Agent requirements are supported by §§ 489.115 and 490.501
and the entity-specific registration forms.

## Scope and remaining manual work

The required state schema includes LPs. Connecticut, Hawaii and Iowa LP entries
contain official applications/fee information only, with no inherited LLC or
corporate safe harbors. Visible disclosures require a separate LP statutory
review for penalties, activity exemptions and reporting. Idaho and Indiana use
reviewed uniform statutes for all three entity types. The published build
contains the requested LLC and C-Corp guides only.

Historical penalty totals, taxes, optional services, electronic checkout charges
and facts outside modeled safe harbors remain applicant-specific. No failed
retrieval is accepted as evidence, and no third-party statutory publisher is
needed for this batch.
