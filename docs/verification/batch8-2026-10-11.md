## Rhode Island

- LLC registration: R.I. Gen. Laws 7-16-49; exemptions and court consequences:
  7-16-54; fees: 7-16-65; annual report: 7-16-66. Registration is $150.
- Corporate authority: 7-1.2-1401 and application 1405; consequences: 1418;
  annual report: 1501; filing/license fees: 1602. Ordinary authority is a
  **$310 minimum**, comprising $150 filing and $160 license fee for fewer than
  75 million authorized shares represented in Rhode Island. At 75 million or more,
  the statutory license fee is one-fifth cent per represented authorized share.
  The represented-share calculation is fact-dependent; $310 is not a flat total.
  Current Form 150, revised March 2026, includes its license-fee worksheet.
- Both requested types have a February 1–May 1 annual-report window, starting in
  the calendar year after registration/authority. Base report fee is $50; the
  $25 late penalty follows the statutory 30-day grace period and SOS applies it
  June 1. Current agency report guidance lists $2.50 online access or $3 for a
  late filing. These are separate from the base fee.
- Current registration instructions, Form 450 and Form 150 require home evidence
  of good standing/legal existence dated within 60 days. Resident/registered
  agents and actual Rhode Island addresses are sourced to 7-16-11, 7-1.2-501
  and agency registration instructions. Virtual/mail-drop addresses are excluded.
- Agency guidance describes a separate minimum annual $400 tax for registered
  for-profit entities even without activity/profit, with no proration. The guide
  distinguishes it from the SOS report and leaves the full tax amount/deadline
  to entity-specific Division of Taxation review.
- LLCs and corporations have nonrepeated, 30-day isolated-transaction exemptions;
  solicitation requires out-of-state acceptance. Corporate unauthorized-business
  liability includes unpaid fees/franchise taxes and applicable penalties.
  Sanctions are manual estimates, not invented exact totals.
- The older cost-table LP annual-report N/A cell is not used to conclude that LPs
  have no reports; the current report page includes partnerships. LP coverage is
  limited to independently sourced registration and its $100 fee.

## South Carolina

- LLC application/exemptions/consequences: S.C. Code 33-44-1002/1003/1008,
  with injunction authority in 1009. Filing is $110 under 33-44-1204(a)(4).
  Income-producing real/tangible property is expressly business subject to the
  excluded holdings; that LLC language is not copied to corporations.
- Corporate authority/application: 33-15-101/103; penalty: 102. Ordinary initial
  authority totals $135: $10 filing under 33-1-220(a)(19), $100 statutory charge
  under 33-1-220(d)(4), and $25 minimum license fee under current 12-20-50.
  The old Chapter 19 reference in the corporation fee provision is read alongside
  current Chapter 20, not a nonexistent Chapter 19 page.
- Corporate initial report **CL-1** and minimum license fee accompany authority
  under 12-20-40. Annual reports go to **DOR**, under 33-16-220 and 12-20-20,
  ordinarily the 15th day of the fourth month after taxable-year end (April 15
  for a calendar-year C corporation). The ordinary license fee is $15 plus $1
  per $1,000 or fraction of capital stock and paid-in/capital surplus, minimum
  $25, subject to apportionment and special-industry rules. The model leaves
  the variable fee unquantified; payment is not extended by a report extension.
- Ordinary LLCs have no routine SOS annual report in Chapter 44. This does not
  imply exemption from taxes, licenses or DOR duties for an LLC taxed as a corporation.
- SOS rejection instructions require an authenticated home certificate of existence
  no more than 30 days old. The statutes and FAQ support in-state agent duties.
- Both requested types have the 30-day isolated-transaction exemption. Corporate
  unauthorized-business penalties are $10 per day, capped at $1,000 per year;
  applicable days and other remedies require manual review. The LLC sections do
  not supply this corporate daily fine.
- LP stub uses 33-42-1620 and the $10 fee in 33-42-2040. It does not inherit LLC
  exemptions, report conclusions or penalty provisions.

## South Dakota

- LLC application/exemptions/consequences: SDCL 47-34A-1002/1003/1008/1009;
  report/fees: 211/212 and Chapter 59-11. Corporate authority/application:
  47-1A-1501/1503; consequences: 1502 and 1502.2; fees: 122.
  Corporate 47-1A-1621 is repealed and is not used as the report authority.
- Ordinary foreign LLC/corporate registrations cost **$750 online / $765 paper**.
  Annual reports cost **$55 online / $70 paper**. Current fee schedule, primary
  fee provisions and July 2025 report PDFs agree. The portal's general instruction
  still says $50 for delinquent reports; the guide explicitly discloses that stale
  line and uses the **$55** penalty confirmed by current law, fees and forms.
- Foreign series LLC authority is $800 before applicable paper charges, not the
  ordinary LLC $750. Farming, agricultural land and foreign beneficial ownership
  need additional review and disclosures.
- Current **59-11-25** says the first report is due before February 1 (January 31)
  in the calendar year after authorization, and the same date thereafter. The
  retrieved page also contains text **effective January 1, 2027** permitting an
  election between the formation-anniversary month and January 31, with a
  change-of-filing-date procedure. The guide preserves that effective-date boundary
  and directs filers to confirm their entity's assigned date with SOS. The portal
  instructs users to look up that date; it supplies no contrary universal deadline.
- Home original one-page certificates of existence or similar authenticated evidence
  are required by statutes/forms; no unsupported numeric freshness is invented.
  Commercial/noncommercial agents and officeholder designations are sourced to
  59-11-6/7 and current authority forms.
- Both requested types have 30-day nonrepeated isolated-transaction exemptions.
  LLC income-producing property triggers are not copied to corporations, whose
  statute exempts ownership without more. Corporate civil penalties are **$100
  per day capped at $1,000 per year**, unlike South Carolina's $10 daily figure.
  LLC court-access/service/injunction provisions are separately represented.
- LP stub uses the independently sourced $125 registration fee, without inherited
  LLC/corporate exemptions, reporting or penalties.

## Tennessee

- Revised LLC Act application: Tenn. Code Ann. 48-249-904; exemptions: 902;
  sanctions: 913; annual report: 1017; agent: 109. These rules do not purport to
  review legacy LLCs still governed by the older LLC Act.
- Corporate authority/exemptions: 48-25-101; sanctions: 102; reports: 48-26-203;
  agent: 48-25-107. Actual statutory text is retrieved from Justia and classified
  as **secondary_statute** in the manifest; the primary-review flag is not cleared.
- Official SS-4233 requires **$50 per member existing at registration**, minimum
  $300 and maximum $3,000. Annual reports use the member-based $300–$3,000 fee
  described by SOS; their variable fee is unquantified in the model. Corporate
  SS-4431 authority costs $600 and annual reports $20. Agent/office changes add $20.
- Statutes and official form instructions set the ordinary annual-report date at
  the first day of the fourth month after fiscal-year end (April 1 for a calendar
  year), subject to an SOS date set by rule. Current TNCaB account/filing instructions
  are linked. Franchise/excise taxes and special elections are separate reviews.
- Both forms require home certificates dated no more than **two calendar months**
  before filing, not an assumed 60 days. Forms additionally require a Tennessee
  DOR Letter of Good Standing when the disclosed commencement date is more than
  one year before approval; this condition appears in the evidence text.
- Both entity exemptions use **one calendar month**, not 30 days. Since the model
  accepts duration only in days, isolated transactions conservatively return manual
  review; no false day-count exemption is encoded.
- LLC unauthorized-business fine is three times the otherwise required filing fee
  for each year or part of a year. Corporate liability is treble fees, penalties
  and taxes plus interest, not merely three times the $600 fee. Payment and court
  restrictions remain explicit; neither formula is turned into an exact estimate.
- LP coverage is an independently sourced $600 filing/fee stub only.

## Utah

- The recodification crosswalk and primary text confirm the shared Title 16,
  Chapter 1a provisions are **effective October 1, 2026**, before this review.
  LLC/corporate foreign registration now uses **16-1a-503/504**, exemptions **506**,
  annual reports **212**, and agent/address provisions **403/404**. Former
  48-3a-902 and the old corporate chapter are not the operative guide citations.
- Section 504(3) now requires **both entity types** to attach a home certificate
  of existence or similar authorized document dated within **90 days**. Older
  no-certificate foreign-LLC advice is not used.
- Official agency fee schedule lists $59 registration and $18 annual report/renewal
  for both types. The starred $18 fee already includes a $5 single-sign-on portal
  surcharge, which is not charged a second time in the guide. Late renewal adds $10.
- Section 212 defines the foreign anniversary month by Utah authority taking
  effect, with the ordinary report due on its **last day**, unless a different
  period is prescribed by rule; filing may start 60 days earlier.
- Section 506(l) exempts an isolated transaction **outside the ordinary course of
  business**, without a day cap. The model does not encode ordinary-course facts,
  so the rule conservatively requires manual review for that activity instead of
  treating every nonrepeated transaction as exempt. Reviewed statutory coverage
  remains distinct from the conservative screening limitation.
- A consenting commercial/noncommercial agent or office/position designation is
  permitted; an outside registered-agent vendor is not universally required.
  Street/mailing addresses are governed by the shared chapter.
- Section 503 restricts court access while preserving contracts, defenses and
  specified limited-liability protections. No invented fixed qualification fine.
  LP coverage is limited to its independently sourced $70 registration fee.
