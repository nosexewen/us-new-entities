## Nebraska

- LLC: §§ 21-156 and 21-157 establish authority and exemptions. Income-producing
  real or tangible personal property triggers business, subject to the security
  interest exception. Nonrecurring isolated transactions must finish in 30 days.
- Corporation: §§ 21-2,203–205 separately establish authority, exemptions and
  consequences. The SOS corporate application requires an authenticated home
  certificate no more than 60 days old; no numeric freshness limit is imported
  into the LLC rule.
- LLC qualification is $120 paper or $110 electronic, including the $10
  certificate surcharge (§ 21-192 and SOS forms). Corporate qualification is
  $110 paper or $100 electronic. These are base fees; optional services and
  payment processing are separate.
- LLC reports are biennial in odd years, January 1–April 1, first due in the odd
  year following the registration year (§ 21-125). Statutory fees are $30 paper
  or $25 electronic; processing may add charges. SOS identifies June 16 delinquency.
- Foreign corporate reports and occupation tax are biennial in even years, due
  March 1 and delinquent April 15. Occupation tax is twice the § 21-303 schedule
  measured by Nebraska property, capped at $30,000 (§ 21-306); no flat fee is invented.
- Corporate unauthorized-business penalties are $500 per day, capped at $10,000
  for each year (§ 21-2,204), requiring manual year allocation. LLC § 21-162
  restricts court access but supplies no fixed monetary qualification fine.

## Nevada

- LLC authority and exemptions: NRS 86.544 and 86.5483. Corporate authority and
  exemptions: 80.010 and 80.015. Both retain the nonrecurring 30-day exemption.
  Solicitation requires outside-Nevada solicitation, receipt and acceptance and
  shipment of goods into Nevada; the broader activity label remains manual review.
- LLC registration is $75. Corporate qualification under 80.050/78.760 is based
  on authorized stock, minimum $75 and maximum $35,000; the guide explicitly
  labels its displayed amount a minimum.
- Initial lists are $150. Annual LLC lists are $150; annual corporate lists are
  stock-based with a $150 minimum (86.5461/80.110). Initial lists are due at
  registration, subject to valid statutory alternative filing dates. Annual
  lists are due by the last day of the anniversary month unless a valid
  alternative applies.
- Separate state business licenses under 76.100/76.130 are $200 for LLCs and $500
  for corporations unless a statutory exemption applies. Base initial totals
  are $425 for LLCs and at least $725 for corporations. The license obligation
  is separate from the annual list; 76.110 supplies a $100 annual late-license penalty.
- Foreign corporations separately publish an annual statement in two issues of a
  Nevada newspaper with at least 1,000 weekly circulation (80.190), by the end
  of March or the third month after fiscal-year close. Charges vary; failure
  incurs $100 per month unpublished. Annual corporate lists are capped at $11,125.
- Willful unauthorized business carries $1,000–$10,000 fines (86.548/80.055).
  The litigation provision allowing 45 days to cure for extraordinary remedies
  is disclosed as a limited exception, not a general qualification grace period.
- Registered agents are reviewed under Chapter 77. Applications declare home
  existence/good standing; no unsupported numeric certificate freshness is added.

## New Hampshire

- LLC registration and exemptions: RSA 304-C:175/174. Corporate authority and
  exemptions: 293-A:15.03/15.01. Full official chapters confirm both entities'
  30-day nonrecurring isolated-transaction exemptions.
- Each foreign registration costs $100. Applications affirm home good standing;
  current statutes do not impose the older corporate certificate attachment rule.
- Annual reports cost $100 and are due January 1–April 1; registrations from
  December 1 of the preceding year through April 1 of the current year skip the
  current-year report (304-C:194/293-A:16.21). The $50 late fee and separate $2
  online handling charge are disclosed.
- 304-C:180 and 293-A:15.02 restrict proceedings until registration and recover
  unauthorized-period fees and penalties. They do not supply a fixed daily fine.

## New Jersey

- LLC provisions: N.J.S.A. 42:2C-58/59/65/26. The LLC exemption list includes a
  30-day isolated transaction; income-producing property has its own trigger.
- Corporate provisions: 14A:13-3/4/11. Corporate exemptions are narrower; no LLC
  isolated-transaction, solicitation or interstate-commerce exemption is copied.
  Corporate certificates must be dated within 30 days before filing; this
  requirement is not automatically applied to LLCs.
- Current Revenue fee guidance lists $100 foreign LLC/corporate qualification;
  the registration page is updated July 1, 2026. The August 2025 legacy packet's
  $125 figure is superseded for pricing, while its registered-office street
  instructions corroborate the agent summary. Annual reports are $75, due in
  the registry's assigned anniversary month; agency guidance describes formation
  month, so foreign registrants are told to confirm the assigned month.
- NJ-REG tax/employer registration is separate. The CBA-1 notice for certain
  foreign corporations disclaiming qualification/tax liability also requires
  independent review; it is not imposed universally on registered corporations.
- Corporate penalties range from $200 to $1,000 for each calendar year within
  a five-year lookback plus costs. LLC § 42:2C-65 supplies court consequences,
  not this corporate monetary formula.
- Justia reproductions are identified as such. The legislative gateway returned
  navigation only; a bill reprint was not accepted as enacted current law.
  Primary review is satisfied by the user’s explicit acceptance of the Justia
  reproductions on 2026-10-11; the guides retain secondary-publisher disclosure.

## New Mexico

- LLC registration/exemptions/penalties: NMSA 1978 53-19-48/54/53. Corporate
  authority/exemptions/penalties: 53-17-1/20. Both preserve the 30-day
  nonrecurring isolated-transaction exemption.
- Certificates must be current within 30 days and unexpired when received;
  registered-agent acceptance is required (53-19-48/53-17-6).
- LLC registration has a $100 statutory base fee (53-19-63). Corporate qualification
  is $1 per 1,000 authorized shares represented in New Mexico, minimum $200 and
  maximum $1,000 (53-2-1(A)(13)). Minimums are not presented as flat final totals.
  Current payable portal totals and processing charges remain unverified.
- Corporate initial report: within 30 days of authority. Later reports: biennial,
  fifteenth day of the fourth month after tax-year end (53-5-2). Report fee is $25
  (53-2-1(A)(16)). The corporate duty is not copied to LLCs; absence of a modeled
  LLC report reflects no periodic/annual LLC reporting requirement under Chapter
  53, Article 19, with a $0 periodic-report fee. Domestic formation is a one-time
  $50 fee under 53-19-63(A); foreign registration stays $100 under (K). Separate
  taxes and licenses remain outside this reporting conclusion.
- LLC civil exposure is up to $200 per year or part plus arrears; corporate civil
  exposure is $200 for each offense plus arrears. These require manual assessment.
- The SOS statutory index was retrieved but its substantive NM OneSource links
  returned 403. On 2026-10-11 the user accepted the Justia reproductions as
  sufficient verification, resolving the primary-review flag without claiming
  independent primary-publisher retrieval. The user also resolved the LLC
  periodic-report limitation; the portal-total limitation remains open.
