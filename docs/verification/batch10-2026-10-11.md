## Filing and annual-report requirements

- [Corps. & Ass'ns § 1-203](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gca&section=1-203&enactments=false):
  **$100 base** foreign LLC registration and corporate intrastate qualification
  under subsection (b)(3)(i); ordinary **$300 annual report** under (b)(3)(ii).
  The retrieved page contains both the earlier version and the version effective
  **October 1, 2026**. Reviewed foreign filing/report amounts are unchanged;
  this review is after that effective date. Domestic formation has a $100 base
  processing fee, but domestic stock-corporation capitalization/organization
  charges remain separate. Foreign guides do not claim a universal $100 domestic
  formation total or include optional expedite/portal charges in the base fee.
- [LLC § 4A-1002](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gca&section=4a-1002&enactments=false)
  requires registration before interstate, intrastate or foreign business in
  Maryland, with acceptable proof of home-jurisdiction good standing. Current
  [foreign LLC form](https://dat.maryland.gov/Documents/Accessible%20Documents/Charter%20-%20Foreign%20Businesses/Foreign%20Limited%20Liability%20Company%20Registration_0326-A.pdf)
  corroborates **$100** and certificate issuance **within the last 60 days**.
- Corporate [§ 7-202](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gca&section=7-202&enactments=false)
  governs interstate/foreign-business registration unless already qualified;
  [§ 7-203](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gca&section=7-203&enactments=false)
  governs intrastate qualification. Current [corporate qualification form](https://dat.maryland.gov/Documents/Accessible%20Documents/Charter%20-%20Foreign%20Businesses/Foreign%20Corporation%20Qualification_0326-A.pdf)
  corroborates **$100** and a home existence/good-standing equivalent **no more
  than 60 days old at filing**. Sixty days is document freshness, not a renewal cycle.
- [Tax–Property § 11-101](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gtp&section=11-101&enactments=false)
  requires annual reports **on or before April 15** from registered/qualified
  foreign LLCs and corporations. Apply its in-effect text, not the alternate
  **September 30, 2029** version also reproduced on the page.
- Current [Form 1](https://dat.maryland.gov/Documents/Accessible%20Documents/BPP%20-%20Annual%20Reports%20and%20Returns/2026%20Filings%20-%20Form%201%20Business%20Entity%20Annual%20Report_0426-A.pdf),
  [instructions](https://dat.maryland.gov/Documents/Accessible%20Documents/BPP%20-%20Annual%20Reports%20and%20Returns/2026%20Form%201%20Instructions%20%20FINAL_0416-A.pdf),
  and [forms index](https://dat.maryland.gov/pages/sdatforms.aspx) confirm the
  ordinary **$300** LLC/stock-corporation report fee, separate from any property
  assessment/tax. Follow applicable Form 1 questions to determine personal-property
  return requirements; no-property status does not itself waive the annual report.
- **Exceptions disclosed:** Form 1 lists a **$0 foreign interstate corporation**
  category; the ordinary $300 intrastate-qualified stock-corporation amount must
  not be assumed for that distinct category. MarylandSaves/employer savings-plan
  waivers and certified-family-farm fees require eligibility evidence.
- SDAT allows an **online-requested 60-day extension**; the forms index specifies
  **June 15 for 2026**. The underlying April 15 duty remains the default. Follow
  the applicable report year and any approved extension.
- The 2026 instruction PDF retains a January 1, 2025 reference in one property
  attestation sentence, unlike the current form's January 1, 2026 reference. The
  rule does not encode an attestation year from that sentence; it directs readers
  to the applicable tax-year form. The fee schedule linked by SDAT is labeled
  **revised May 2024**; current statute and March 2026 foreign forms corroborate
  reviewed $100 fees, and no fully revised 2026 schedule is claimed.

## Screening exemptions and resident agents

- LLC [§ 4A-1009](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gca&section=4a-1009&enactments=false)
  and corporate [§ 7-103](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gca&section=7-103&enactments=false)
  exempt internal affairs, bank accounts and isolated transactions outside a
  course of similar transactions. **Neither isolated exemption has a fixed day
  cap**; no 30-day rule is invented. Solicitation is not inherited as a reviewed
  exemption. Other nonenumerated activities remain fact-specific.
- Corporate interstate-only commerce is exempt from **intrastate qualification**
  under § 7-103, while **registration still applies under § 7-202**. LLC § 4A-1002
  also expressly includes interstate business. The filing-duty screen therefore
  triggers for Maryland business even when commerce is interstate only; the guide
  explains that this decision includes registration rather than only qualification.
- LLC § 4A-1009(b) treats income-producing real/tangible property as business,
  except the foreclosure/default-acquired property exclusions in (a). Those
  factual exclusions require manual review. No LLC property trigger is copied
  into the corporation rule.
- [§ 1-101(x)](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gca&section=1-101&enactments=false)
  defines eligible private agents as Maryland residents or Maryland corporations,
  LLCs or LPs. A private agent needs consent and an actual Maryland address;
  current foreign LLC form excludes P.O. boxes.
- **Private appointment is optional.** Both current foreign forms appoint SDAT
  when no private agent is named or it cannot be found/served, **for no additional
  fee**. Corporate §§ 7-202/7-203 permit express Department appointment; LLC
  § 4A-1002 requires the fallback statement. Statutory service is distinct from
  hiring a commercial provider. Domestic LLC § 4A-210 is not used to impose a
  Maryland principal office on foreign LLCs.
- Guide structured data names **Maryland Department of Assessments and Taxation**
  as the filing agency. Optional-agent FAQ text now uses each state's rule notes
  instead of hard-coded New York wording; Maryland does not name another state's
  service agent.

## Nonregistration sanctions and limits

- LLC [§ 4A-1007](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gca&section=4a-1007&enactments=false):
  **$200 Department penalty**, reducible/abatable under Tax–Property § 14-704;
  current LLC form requires it with registration when prior unregistered business
  is disclosed. Each member and transacting agent may face a **separate misdemeanor
  conviction fine of at most $1,000**. Court access requires penalty payment and
  compliance, or the statutory company/successor cessation alternative. Contracts
  and defense remain valid; unregistered business appoints SDAT for service.
- Corporations: [§ 7-301](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gca&section=7-301&enactments=false)
  supplies court-access cure/cessation alternatives and
  [§ 7-302](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gca&section=7-302&enactments=false)
  imposes **$200**, reducible/abatable, with **up to $1,000 on misdemeanor
  conviction** for each officer/transacting agent. LLC member sanctions are not
  copied into the corporate rule.
- These are **nonregistration/nonqualification penalties**, not $200 per day,
  per year, or a universal annual-report late fee. Report forfeiture/property-return
  penalties depend on separate facts. Overall penalty estimates remain manual to
  avoid asserting an exact total despite reduction/abatement or criminal proceedings.
  § 7-303 concerns subsequent § 7-206 compliance and is excluded from the initial
  qualification penalty model.
- LP registration is **$100** under § 1-203(b)(3)(i), corroborated by the linked
  SDAT schedule. Its exemptions, agent duties, reports and sanctions require
  separate entity-specific review; the empty stub report list does not mean no duties.
